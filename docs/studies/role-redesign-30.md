# Driver, ORM and MCP role candidates (issues #30 and #23)

Date: 2026-10-25, against `main` at [`4e1281c`](https://github.com/cubrid-lab/brand/commit/4e1281c). Renderer: librsvg (`rsvg-convert`), composited with Pillow.

These are **study candidates for maintainer review**. Nothing here is approved, and no file in `src/`, `dist/` or `registry/` changes: `pycubrid` still uses the current Driver icon and `sqlalchemy-cubrid` the current ORM icon until a candidate is chosen and promoted in a separate change.

## Directions being implemented

- **Driver: vertical stack (top to bottom).** Approved direction in issue #30: the existing layered geometry, reassigned to the Driver role.
- **ORM: lateral stack (side by side).** Approved direction in issue #30. The final geometry needs review, so there are three candidates.
- **MCP: "3-Way Router".** Selected in issue #23 (candidate A). No vector source for candidate A was found in this repository's history, branches, pull requests or issue attachments, so the two drawings below are reconstructions from the written description: a three-way connection worked into the cube's planes and negative space. If the original comparison image exists elsewhere, please compare it with these.
- **Color:** Python Blue `#3776AB`, one color, for every Python repository (issue #30, final color decision). No yellow or green. Each candidate is also rendered in one non-Python language token to show that only the color changes.
- **Unchanged:** the three-color organization emblem and every other role.

- The Driver candidate's drawing is identical to the current `src/orm.svg`.
- Every candidate differs from every current role.
- Candidates must be registered `study` entries.

### Observations by eye (one reviewer, rsvg; not a design decision)

- **Driver stack** reads the same as today's ORM at every size, by construction. Once it is approved, the ORM role must move to one of the lateral candidates, or the two roles would be identical.
- **`orm-lateral-plates`** is the clearest "same idea, other axis" partner for the Driver stack, and it keeps its shape down to 16 px. Its silhouette is not the full cube.
- **`orm-lateral-slabs`** keeps the full cube silhouette. Its three right-face strips blur together at 16 px, and they read as two or three bars at 24 px.
- **`orm-mirrored-rows`** carries the clearest mapping metaphor at 48 px and up. At 16 px its rows blur together, and at 24 px they can read as horizontal layers, close to the Driver stack.
- **`mcp-router-hub`** reads as a three-way junction from 16 px on light and dark. It is the more literal router.
- **`mcp-router-channels`** is the most integrated with the cube's negative space and has no white mark. At 16 px the ring and seams merge into a small Y. On dark backgrounds the channels show the page color, so the router is quieter there.
