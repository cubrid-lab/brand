# Python Yellow contrast treatments (issue #38)

Date: 2026-10-25, against `main` at [`4a0555e`](https://github.com/cubrid-lab/brand/commit/4a0555e). Renderer: librsvg (`rsvg-convert`), composited with Pillow.

The maintainer decided on 2026-10-25 in [issue #38](https://github.com/cubrid-lab/brand/issues/38) to change the Python language token from Python Blue `#3776AB` to Python Yellow `#FFD43B`. That supersedes the "Python Blue" color decision recorded in [`role-redesign-30.md`](role-redesign-30.md). Yellow has about 1.43:1 contrast on white, so this study compared contrast treatments before any approved asset changed.

## Decision (2026-10-25)

The maintainer (@yeongseon) reviewed [`family.png`](python-yellow-38/family.png) and [`checks.md`](python-yellow-38/checks.md), generated at [`31adfc4`](https://github.com/cubrid-lab/brand/commit/31adfc43b48c3b5249774ca75a7247022472762c) on [PR #40](https://github.com/cubrid-lab/brand/pull/40), and selected **`t3-ochre-keyline`**: dark ink marks and an ochre keyline on all four Python icons.

`tokens/colors.json` now sets Python to `#FFD43B` with that treatment, and `scripts/build.py` draws it for every Python icon ([construction](../GEOMETRY.md#light-token-treatment-issue-38)). The promoted `dist/icons/` SVGs draw exactly what the `t3-ochre-keyline` candidates draw; `tests/test_python_yellow_candidates.py` checks this, and the renders match pixel for pixel at 16 to 128 px. [`python-yellow-38/promoted-before-after.png`](python-yellow-38/promoted-before-after.png) shows every generated icon and the organization emblem before and after: only the four Python icons change. The decision is recorded in [PROVENANCE.md](../../PROVENANCE.md#python-yellow-with-an-ochre-keyline-issue-38).

The review sheets below are kept as reviewed: their "current" row shows Python Blue. Rerunning the study script on a later revision shows the then-current icons in that row.

## Study

The shapes are the Driver, ORM and MCP geometry selected in PR #37 and the existing Cookbook mark. TypeScript, Go, Rust, Neutral, the reserved JavaScript token and the organization emblem are unchanged.

## Candidates

Defined in [`tokens/python-yellow-candidates.json`](../../tokens/python-yellow-candidates.json) and generated by `scripts/build.py` into [`studies/python-yellow/`](../../studies/python-yellow/) (one SVG per treatment and Python repository). Two inks are derived from the yellow: **dark** `#40350F` (yellow mixed 75% toward black) and **ochre** `#8C7520` (45% toward black). A keyline is 0.6 viewBox units wide (0.6 px at 24 px) and lies outside the shape: around every layer for Driver and ORM, and clipped to the outside of the cube for MCP and Cookbook, so the seams stay open.

| Id | Marks (MCP, Cookbook) | Keyline | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `t0-naive` | white | none | Reference only: the token substituted with no other change. |
| `t1-dark-marks` | dark ink | ochre, Driver and ORM only | MCP and Cookbook rely on their dark marks; layered icons get an edge. |
| `t2-dark-keyline` | dark ink | dark ink, all four | The strongest outline on white; nearly invisible on dark, where the yellow faces carry the shape. |
| `t3-ochre-keyline` | dark ink | ochre, all four | T1 made uniform across the four Python icons. |

## Measurements

WCAG relative-luminance contrast, used as a yardstick, not a compliance claim. Light is `#FFFFFF`, dark is `#0D1117`. Face shades are the generator's (top +8% white, left +10% black, right plain); Driver and ORM layers are unshaded.

| Treatment | Lowest mark-on-face contrast (MCP, Cookbook) | Driver/ORM outer edge on light / dark | MCP/Cookbook outer edge on light / dark | Yellow faces on light / dark | Mark samples within 0.3 of a seam | Marks under 1 unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `t0-naive` | 1.39 | 1.43 / 13.28 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 0 | 0 |
| `t1-dark-marks` | 6.84 | 4.49 / 4.22 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 0 | 0 |
| `t2-dark-keyline` | 6.84 | 12.11 / 1.56 | 12.11 / 1.56 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 0 | 0 |
| `t3-ochre-keyline` | 6.84 | 4.49 / 4.22 | 4.49 / 4.22 | 1.39 / 10.70 | 0 | 0 |

For comparison, the white mark on the current Python Blue faces is 4.16:1 at its lowest. On a dark page the yellow faces themselves (10.70:1 or more) carry every silhouette, so a keyline's dark-page contrast matters little there. `tests/test_python_yellow_candidates.py` checks in CI that the treatments only change the mark color and add a keyline, that dark marks reach 3:1 on every face and stay clear of the seams, and that keylines reach 3:1 on white.

### Observations by eye (one reviewer, rsvg; not a design decision)

- **Yellow is clearly distinct** from TypeScript blue and Go cyan in every treatment, at 24 and 48 px.
- **T0** loses the white MCP and Cookbook marks on light backgrounds at every size, and the yellow layers fade into white at 16 and 24 px. Not shippable as is.
- **Dark marks (T1–T3)** read at 24 px and up on both backgrounds. On dark pages the dark mark and the open seams are both dark, so at 16 px the MCP hub and its links merge with the seams; at 24 px the router still reads.
- **T1** keeps the MCP and Cookbook silhouettes as soft as T0 on white (only their marks give them shape), so the family looks mixed: outlined layers next to unoutlined cubes.
- **T2** gives the crispest silhouettes on white at all sizes and reads as a deliberate outline style. At 16 px on white, the dark lines between Driver/ORM layers make the layers busier. It is the largest departure from the flat look of the other languages.
- **T3** is the quietest consistent option: ochre edges hold the shapes on white from 24 px, look close to face shading on dark, and stay nearest to the flat family style. At 16 px on white the ochre edge and the layer gaps blend into a striped block.
- 16 px remains a limitation for the Driver and ORM layers on white in every treatment.

## Known limits

At 16 px the Driver and ORM layers blur on white, and on dark pages the MCP hub merges with the dark seams. Both read from 24 px. Downstream repositories repin the new icons in their own PRs.
